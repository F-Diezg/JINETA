Jineta

Engagement scenarios

Iris quadrotors against incoming threats, simulated in 6-DOF at 100 Hz. Jineta is built to compare how a swarm of interceptors can be coordinated; these pages are the baseline the strategies will be measured against: every Iris knows where its threat is, flies straight at it (pure pursuit) and is assigned it by hand in the scenario file, with no coordination between them. Each page replays its cases in 3D.

__MENU_TALLY__

  • Captured
  • Missed or escaped
  • Target lost to another interceptor
  • Still open at the end
__MENU_CARDS__
Every track, range and time is simulation output. A page that is not there yet is written by the command on its row; python -m examples.run_scenarios writes them all.
